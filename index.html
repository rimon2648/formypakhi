to my Pakhi
Pakhi, I'm so sorry for the way I spoke to you. My words came out wrong and I hate that they hurt you — especially when all you were doing was being honest with me about how you felt.

Your insecurities are never a burden to me. I should have held them gently, not pushed them away.

Your face, your eyes, every single part of you — I find beautiful. Acne doesn't change what I see when I look at you. I see the bold, straight-talking, incredible girl I am so lucky to have.

I'm sorry. Truly.
— always yours 🌸
what I promise you
🤍 To hold your feelings with care, always.
👁️ To remind you how beautiful you are — every day.
🌿 To choose my words more gently, even when I'm frustrated.
🫂 To be your safe place, not a source of hurt.

"Your eyes tell stories I never want to stop reading.
And your boldness? That's one of my favourite things about you."

made with love & a very sorry heart 💕
